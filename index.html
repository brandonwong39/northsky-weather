About NorthSky

NorthSky displays Canadian weather radar and short-term extrapolation from MSC GeoMet. For official warnings and forecasts, visit Environment and Climate Change Canada.

This is an informational weather site, not an official warning service. Radar and forecasts may be delayed or unavailable.
